## Live verification notes

The screenshots show successfully loaded feature pages. During the same production walkthrough, two list-to-detail actions did not complete successfully:

- Opening a lab result from the patient lab-results list returned “Lab order not found or you don’t have access.”
- Opening an invoice from the patient billing list returned “Invoice not found or you don’t have access.”

Those error states are not represented as completed features in this gallery and should be investigated separately.
